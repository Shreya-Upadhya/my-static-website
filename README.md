## 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

### 👥 VS HUMAN MODE

```text
PLAYER X  →  PLAYER O
    ↓             ↓
 Take Turns & Get 3 In A Row
```

Win by matching:

```text
❌ ❌ ❌
⭕ ⭕ ⭕
```

Horizontally, Vertically, or Diagonally.

---

### 🤖 VS AI MODE

```text
YOU = ❌
AI  = ⭕
```

The AI uses the **Minimax Algorithm** and always chooses the best possible move.

⚠️ Warning: The AI never loses.
